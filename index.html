How to Play

W A S DMove MouseLook around (arrow keys also work) SpaceJump / swim up Space ×2Creative: toggle flying (Space up, Shift down) CtrlSprint (or double-tap W) ShiftSneak — won’t walk off edges Left clickHold to mine · click to attack Right clickPlace block · eat food · use workbench 1–9 / WheelSelect hotbar slot EInventory & crafting QDrop item (Ctrl+Q drops the stack) Middle clickPick the block you’re looking at F5Switch camera: first person, behind, in front Right clickOn a furnace: smelt · on a bed: sleep · with armour: equip TChat (in a party) F1 · F3Hide HUD · debug info EscPause

The world is endless: walk in any direction and new land keeps generating. In Creative you have every block, can fly and break anything instantly. In Survival, keep your hunger up with apples and wild berries, light torches before nightfall, and watch your step — long falls hurt.
