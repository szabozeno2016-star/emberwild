The Realms

The EmberdeepA burning underworld of cinderstone, lava seas and Scorchlings. Ember Gate = 8 Cobblestone + 4 Ember Dust + 2 Sunvein Ingots (at a Workbench)
The AetherPeaceful islands above the clouds. Nothing here will attack you. Sky Gate = 4 Wool + 4 Glass + 2 Skyshards (at a Workbench)
The HollowThe last realm. Defeat the Hollow King to finish the game. Hollow Gate = 4 Voidglass + 6 Cinder Shards + 3 Cinder Hearts — all found in the Emberdeep
  1. Craft a gate, place it on the ground and right-click it (on a phone: tap it) to travel.
  2. A gate waits for you on the other side. Use it to come back home.
  3. In the Emberdeep: mine Cinder Ore for shards, mine Voidglass beside the lava with a Sunvein Pickaxe, and defeat Scorchlings for Cinder Hearts.
  4. In the Aether: mine Aurorite and forge the Aurora Blade, the strongest sword — it helps against the King.
  5. In Creative mode all three gates are in the catalogue under Realms.

The world is endless: walk in any direction and new land keeps generating. In Creative you have every block, can fly and break anything instantly. In Survival, keep your hunger up with apples and wild berries, light torches before nightfall, and watch your step — long falls hurt. Build a house with a door, keep your treasure in chests, and climb down mines on ladders. Herds of sheep, cows, pigs and hens roam the grasslands — and don’t hug a cactus.
